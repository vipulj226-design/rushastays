About Rusha Stays

Live Better. Work Smarter. Stay Hassle-Free.

Rusha Stays is a professionally managed accommodation brand offering fully furnished rooms, premium suites, studio residences and managed living spaces across prime locations in Gurugram (Gurgaon).

Founded with a simple vision of making urban living more comfortable, convenient and hassle-free, Rusha Stays was created for professionals who seek more than just a room. We believe accommodation should support your lifestyle, productivity and peace of mind.

Whether you're relocating to Gurugram (Gurgaon), starting a new job, working on a project assignment, building a startup, pursuing an internship or looking for a better living experience, Rusha Stays provides thoughtfully designed accommodations that help you feel at home from day one.

Our Story

Finding quality accommodation in Gurugram (Gurgaon) can often be overwhelming. Between unfurnished rentals, inconsistent services and traditional PG setups, professionals frequently struggle to find a place that offers both comfort and convenience.

Rusha Stays was established to bridge this gap by creating professionally managed living spaces that combine the warmth of a home with the convenience of hospitality.

Over the years, we have evolved into a trusted accommodation partner for working professionals, consultants, startup founders, interns, remote workers and relocating residents seeking safe, comfortable and fully managed living solutions.

Our Vision

To become Gurugram (Gurgaon)’s most trusted managed accommodation brand by redefining urban living through thoughtfully designed spaces, exceptional service and resident-first experiences.

Our Mission

To provide safe, comfortable and professionally managed accommodations that enable residents to live better, work smarter and stay hassle-free.
